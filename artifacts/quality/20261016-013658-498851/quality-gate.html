Model quality gate

Each model must pass every check in calm, breezy and gusty weather. Landing checks use the lower end of the 95% Wilson confidence interval, rather than the success rate alone. These are explicit project acceptance criteria, not industry standards or real-flight certification.

Results cover one trained policy across simulation episodes. They do not measure training-seed variability. The intentionally hovering model is expected to fail. Models are never deployed or replaced automatically.
