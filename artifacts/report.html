Rocket Landing Lab

PPO learns from eight numbers to choose between four engine actions. These results compare the original reward with three reward-shaping experiments.

Read the original score first. A custom bonus can increase training reward while making landing worse. Each model is tested without custom reward shaping. The same episode seeds are used across models. One training seed is a demonstration, not evidence of a robust improvement.

The environment's +100 terminal reward identifies a resting lander. Failures combine crashes and leaving the bounds. Timeouts are recorded separately. Engine-use cost is a proxy based on the game's penalties, not kilograms of fuel. The 200-point benchmark is an average-return target, not an individual-episode landing test.

What to investigate

Does the hover bonus produce more airborne frames or timeouts? Does the fixed reward still land? Compare the full evaluation CSV and videos; hovering is a possible learned behavior, not a guaranteed result.

The fixed reward removes the hover bonus, adds a small time cost, and uses a discounted potential difference. It is an experiment, not a promise that it beats the original reward.
